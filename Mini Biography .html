INTRODUCTION TO SPACE

Space is the endless area that starts where Earth's atmosphere ends. It is dark, silent, and very cold. Space contains everything - planets, stars, moons, galaxies, and black holes. Space is about 13.8 billion years old. The study of space is calledAstronomy.

OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

We live in the Solar System. In the center is the Sun, which is a big star. It gives us light and heat. Without the Sun, there would be no life. Around the Sun, 8 planets move in fixed paths called orbits.

THE 8 PLANETS OF OUR SOLAR SYSTEM

1. Mercury:It is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun. It has no atmosphere and is very fast. A year on Mercury is only 88 days.

2. Venus: The second planet. It is the hottest planet (462°C) because of thick gases. It is called Earth's twin.

3. Earth:Our home planet. It is the only planet where life exists. 71% of Earth is water. Water is made of H2O. It has one moon.Radius of earth is 6.371X106m.

4. Mars:Known as the Red Planet because of its red soil. Scientists believe there was once water on Mars. NASA sends rovers to Mars.

5. Jupiter:The biggest planet in our solar system. It is made of gas and has a giant red storm.

6. Saturn:Famous for its beautiful rings made of ice and rock. It has more than 80 moons approximately.

7. Uranus:An ice giant that rotates sideways. It has a blue-green color.

8. Neptune:The farthest and windiest planet. It is dark blue and very cold.

9. Pluto:Pluto is a Planet dwarf planet.

INTERSTING FACTS ABOUT SPACE

1. There are more stars in space than grains of sand on all beaches of Earth.

2. One day on Venus is longer than one year on Venus.

3. Footprints on the Moon will stay for millions of years because there is no wind.

4. Black holes are places where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape.

5. The Sun is so big that 13 lakh Earths can fit inside it.

Conclusions

Space is full of mysteries. We know very little about it. Every planet is special and different. By studying planets and space, we learn how important our Earth is. We must take care of our planet because it is our only home.
